[Privacy Policy for YouTube Avemujify]

1. Data Collection
    - This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. No sign-up or login is required to use this extension.

2. Data Storage (Local Storage)
    - The extension uses the browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) solely to save your user preferences (e.g., toggle status, appearance probability). This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any external servers.

3. Permissions Usage
    - Host Permissions (*://*.youtube.com/*): This permission is used exclusively to modify the DOM of YouTube pages to replace thumbnails. The extension does not track or collect your viewing history or activity.
